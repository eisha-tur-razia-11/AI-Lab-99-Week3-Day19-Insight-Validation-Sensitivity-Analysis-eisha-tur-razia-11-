## Priority Sensitivity Test
The priority finding concerns customer revenue concentration.

**Original:** Customer revenue calculated using all valid transactions.

**Alternative:** Exclude the single highest-value transaction and recalculate customer revenue/rankings.

**Conclusion:** If the broad customer-value pattern remains materially similar, the finding is considered stable under the sensitivity test.

## Final Validated Insights
1. High-value customers contribute significantly to revenue — **High Confidence**
2. Product revenue contribution differs across products — **High Confidence**
3. Monthly sales performance varies — **Medium Confidence**
4. Customer segments differ in purchasing value/behavior — **Medium Confidence**
5. Campaign revenue outcomes differ — **Medium Confidence**
